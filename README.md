# Tasks – a simple to-do list for your Garmin watch

Keep your tasks on your wrist. Tasks shows your to-do list at a glance, lets you open any task to read its full name and notes, and mark it as done with a tap.

## Features

- Clean task list that is easy to read on your watch
- Tap a task to see its full name and an optional note
- Mark tasks as done (or undo it) with a tap or the Select button
- Clear all completed tasks with one tap
- Up to 10 tasks, each with an optional note
- Works with touch and button watches

## How to add tasks

Watches don't have a keyboard, so you enter your tasks on your phone:

1. Open the Garmin Connect or Connect IQ app on your phone.
2. Go to your watch > Connect IQ apps > Tasks > Settings.
3. Type your tasks in the "Task" fields and optional details in the "Note" fields.
4. Save and sync your watch. Your list updates after the sync.

Your "done" marks are stored on the watch. Clearing a task from the settings removes it from the list.
